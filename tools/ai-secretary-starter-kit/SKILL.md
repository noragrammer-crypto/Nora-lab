# AI Secretary Starter Kit

When helping with a copy of this kit:

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, then `command-center/now.md`, then `wiki/index.md`.
2. Keep raw inputs in `inbox/`; do not present them as verified long-term knowledge.
3. Create or update one focused wiki concept at a time, with a `type` field and links from the nearest `index.md`.
4. Turn agreed executable work into a GitHub Issue. Do not perform external actions without explicit approval.
5. Keep credentials and private context out of any public output.
