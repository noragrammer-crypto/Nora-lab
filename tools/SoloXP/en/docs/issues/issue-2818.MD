# #2818 [Improvement Recommendation] xp_Auditor ownership base block: undefined if parent story of bug is closed

## Body

## How it was discovered

Found during xp_Reviewer review of `#2807` (xp_Auditor: Story-level AllGREEN audit interlocks with known RED from another story).

## Content

Ownership-based asymmetric blocks were introduced in the Story-level Auditor phase (section 3) of `SoloXP/skills/xp_Auditor/SKILL.md` in `#2807`/`#2809`:

- Bug's parent is **own story** → block (as before)
- Bug's parent is **another "open" story** → Do not block (duplicate detection comments only)
- Bug's parent is **not set** → own story takes ownership and blocks

These three branches do not include the case that the bug's parent story is already **closed**. If you interpret the wording literally, it could fall under "Owned by other stories → not blocked", but the entity (open story) that actually fixes the bug no longer exists. As a result, `[Auditor GREEN]` continues to be recorded without being owned by anyone, potentially permanently missing a real regression.

## Reproduction conditions (assumed)

1. Bug issue X is filed under story A, and story A is closed (X remains unclosed or recurs)
2. Test corresponding to X in Story-level E2E of another story B becomes RED
3. xp_Auditor checks the parent of X (story A) → Story A is already closed
4. Under the current wording, it is classified as "Owned by other stories" and story B is not blocked and `[Auditor GREEN]` is recorded.
5. X is left alone and is not owned by anyone.

## Improvement policy (proposal)

Add "If the parent story is closed" to the branch in section 3:
- Story whose parent is **closed** → Treat it as if it were not set, and the current story reacquires ownership and blocks it (replace with `replace_parent: true`)

## Scope of influence

`SoloXP/skills/xp_Auditor/SKILL.md` (Story-level Auditor Phase 3), `SoloXP/docs/spec/xp_auditor.md`
